# Travel Atlas — editorial reference and concept library
Updated 17 November 2026. Owner-led Tuesday sessions only. *Inspiration*, not authority to reuse external creators' images, text or footage. Find the original news/report/source and independently verify every claim and right before producing.

## New format rule
- Real photo = manually swipeable 1080x1350 Instagram carousel POST, 7–9 genuinely different photographic scenes where available, plus a spectacular licensed-photo **Fill Your Feed With Wonder.** ending. Normally 8–10 total slides, including ending; fewer only if genuinely stronger.
- Real moving video = 1080x1920 REEL, normally 7–10 distinct licensed real clips, strong high-contrast named place and premise title, well-placed on-screen text, moving footage closing brand mark, no sound/music.
- No zoom-pan-from-photo MP4s, no photo slideshow Reels disguised as motion, no “more than just…” or generic “hidden gems” padding. Do not reenable automated ChatGPT jobs.
- Preserve original publication UUID and schedule only after inspection.

## Rotation of editorial formats (choose based on visual evidence, not quotas)

| Approach | Example original hook | User benefit / source approach |
|---|---|---|
| Extraordinary regional destination | “Indonesia beyond Bali: volcano lakes, pink beaches & dragons” | Clear real place, strong inspiring range of images |
| Curiosity-led cross-country comparison | “Where the desert really meets the ocean” | A surprising named phenomenon across countries |
| Saveable transport journey | “Scenic train journeys that are worth the trip alone” | Name actual route, operator, stations and standout view; official rail pages |
| Choose-your-adventure | “Would you hike these ridgelines or ride the train?” | Different ways to experience a region, accurate access and seasonal evidence |
| Cultural experience / event | “The town that transforms into a sea of lanterns” | Real local tradition with reputable tourism/organiser validation |
| Natural phenomena explainer | “Why these crater lakes keep changing colour” | Visual geology with reputable scientific source (e.g., NASA, local park) |
| Seasonal shortlist | “The winter towns worth saving for next year” | Distinct non-repetitive scenes and factual visit appeal |
| Saveable mini itinerary | “A slow weekend in Porto, without a packed checklist” | Useful interesting route over mere generic landmark list |
| Film mini-journey | “Five experiences in Türkiye worth your next trip” | 7+ authentic MOVING clips, editorial story, strong readable hook |
| Atlas oddity / overlooked stop | “The village where almost every home has a grass roof” | Weird genuine place and context, accurate source and licensed photos |
| Photographic story | “From the sea to a mountain in one afternoon” | Contrasts within a single destination with truthful geography |
| Trip theme / interests | “Surf towns that deliver when you’re not in the water” | Experience-led selection, not vague rankings; plan-friendly specificity |

## Original research channels for each manual Tuesday review
- **Atlas Obscura** — odd or overlooked places and the local story behind them. https://www.atlasobscura.com/ . Survey of its own engaged audience identifies articles/guides as useful discovery triggers, but don't generalise those audience results to everyone: https://press.atlasobscura.com/atlas-obscura-travelers-choose-the-overlooked-over-the-obvious/
- **Lonely Planet** — train routes, unusual itineraries, region-specific destination inspiration: https://www.lonelyplanet.com/ . Seek independently confirmed logistics and operators for any factual guide.
- **Time Out Travel** — timely city and experience curation: https://www.timeout.com/travel . Prefer independent destinations/venues and evidence, no copy-pasting rankings.
- **Condé Nast Traveller** — experience-led features and fresh places to watch: https://www.cntraveller.com/ and https://www.cntraveler.com/ .
- **The Culture Trip, BBC Travel, National Geographic Travel** — distinctive cultural story leads; cross-check with local sources, and do not republish their image assets or prose.
- **Instagram travel creators** — examine hook architecture, slide pacing, first-scene legibility, varied location visual quality, useful on-image details, and final brand card. Never download their photos/videos for reposting without explicit permission.
- **Travel creator editorial benchmarking** — reported higher saveability for actionable carousel guides and good stop-scroll place/outcome hooks; treat anecdotal/third-party benchmark data cautiously. Examples: https://topcarousels.com/blog/viral-travel-carousels and https://www.travelagewest.com/industry-insight/business-features/how-to-create-travel-instagram-reels

## Quality gate for planning
Five–eight distinct real locations or activities should always trump generic volume; source media quality and editing matter more than forced numbers. A **photo count of 5 should no longer be treated as a finished ideal**. Audit original free photo pages individually, preserve credits and licence, verify location and exactly rendered files. For reels, audit each original full motion clip, rights and source page, decoded footage, output contact sheet, first title, full playback when accessible and zero audio stream.

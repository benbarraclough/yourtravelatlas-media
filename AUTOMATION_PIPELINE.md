## Three editorial strategies and an initial flexible allocation

The audience-facing **three strategies**, not rigid fixed post templates, are:
1. **Destination discoveries (~40%)** — editorial photo guides to an actual place/region/trip, with compelling imagery, essential experiences, real facts and useful specifics. Faroe Islands is the formal Collection aesthetic; **Japan** is a good visually approachable destination-discovery reference even though its underlying carousel uses the hook-led renderer. Porto is another useful destination-guide example. Vary formal Collection and more playful Discovery presentation within this **same user-facing category**. Do not require every destination post to use Faroe's formal layout.
2. **Attention-grabbing curated lists, seasonal/trending ideas (~40%)** — honest clickable hooks such as most magical villages, best snowboarding destinations, extraordinary national parks, real travel oddities, unusual facts or timely attractions. The content must substantiate the premise, explain the selection criteria for "best", show every promised item, and genuinely warrant saves and shares. Do NOT use misleading/copycat clickbait, fake rankings, or trends irrelevant to beautiful travel discovery. Use the Discovery carousel treatment, with original visual selection and writing.
3. **Authentic moving-footage Reels (~20%)** — exceptional real video with at least five genuinely distinct strong shots, clear hook/location, helpful story/context and silent zero-audio export; post only when clips are fully licensed and compelling.

These 40/40/20 shares are **testable editorial defaults**, not guaranteed slots, rigid quotas or claims of optimal performance. In a fully used 20-post month this is approximately 8 destination discoveries + 8 hook-led lists/trends + 4 Reels. With fewer than 20 truly excellent eligible posts, scale and vary. Log each item's `strategy` separately from underlying renderer `content_type`; this prevents miscounting Japan as a list post. Around once a month evaluate the split with actual analytics (or state if insufficient sample), not gut feel or unverified competitor engagement.

All format standards, asset rights and editorial gates remain mandatory, including correct location, named signature experiences, no public issue numbers, small handle, no music and exact final visual inspection. Actual published items can be classified by intent independently of renderer style.

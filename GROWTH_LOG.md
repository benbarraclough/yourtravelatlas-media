# Your Travel Atlas — growth research and experiments

**Established 8 November 2026.** This is the persistent log for the weekly `Atlas Growth & Strategy` task. Read the active policies in `AUTOMATION_PIPELINE.md`, `EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md`, `CONTENT_PROCESS.md` and `EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md`.

**Objective:** Improve the actual quality, appeal, reach, saves, shares, following, discoverability and usefulness of @yourtravelatlas at zero extra spend, while preserving its creative identity and editorial accuracy. Do not turn it into promotional generic tourism influencer content.

Each dated run should record:
- Actual Metricool and accessible Instagram/account metrics with timeframe, exact availability and limitations; avoid causal or performance claims when the account is new or the sample is too small.
- Competitor and current trend/editorial research with exact posts/URLs inspected and truthful scope, including hospitality.guide and varied original references without copying them.
- Profile clarity, positioning, account bio/description/identity, content pillars, Instagram-friendly covers/first frames, searchable terms and captions, follow/save/share proposition, realistic highlights/pins and series potential. Evaluate user-facing distinction between destination discoveries, hook-led curated lists/trends and authentic silent Reels.
- A maximum of 1–2 evidence-based measurable growth experiments, each with a concrete next action, owner/stage, measurement (prefer saves/reach, shares/reach, profile visits/follows or follower growth where measurable), observation period, and stop/revise conditions. Explicitly separate facts, hypotheses and anecdotal inspiration.
- Feed eligible content ideas into the ordinary `content/production-queue.json` research stage with their source/evidence only after the research gate. Do not claim an experiment launched unless there is an actual change and measurement plan. No unapproved logo/profile changes, paid services, engagement bots, spam, purchased followers or platform-policy violations.
- Finish each run with a brief visible result for the owner's requested notification, even if data is unavailable or no action could be taken.

## Baseline — 8 November 2026

- Current workflow aims for 4–5 exceptional posts/week up to confirmed maximum 20/month; content quality is the higher priority.
- Flexible suggested mix: roughly 40% destination discovery (formal Faroe, approachable Japan), 40% honest attention-grabbing lists/trends, 20% silent real-video Reels.
- 5 November Metricool inspection confirmed Porto, French Riviera and Iceland published, with Japan due 9 November; early post-level analytics were too limited for firm comparisons. Recheck live sources, don't assume this is current.
- Before any optimisation experiment, verify that the fundamental automated workflow can actually produce, visually review and schedule its first new edition correctly.

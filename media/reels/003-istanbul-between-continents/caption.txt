One long weekend. Two continents. A city full of reasons to stay out a little longer. 🇹🇷

In Istanbul, even getting from one neighbourhood to another can become part of the adventure. Follow the red tram along İstiklal Avenue, linger by the market stalls and make time for a proper Turkish coffee. Then take a public ferry across the Bosphorus, order tea by the window and watch the city unfold from the water.

Don't try to tick off every monument. Leave space for wandering, neighbourhood cafés and an evening beside the strait. Check current ferry routes before you set off.

Who would you take for a long weekend between Europe and Asia? 🌊

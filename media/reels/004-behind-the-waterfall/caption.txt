Most waterfalls ask you to stand in front of them. This one lets you see the world through the water. 🇮🇸

📍 Seljalandsfoss, South Iceland
A 60-metre waterfall just off the Ring Road, with a footpath that passes behind the falling water. When the path is open, follow it into the rock recess and look back out across the landscape through the spray—that’s the view to come for.

Bring a waterproof jacket and shoes with grip: the path gets wet. Plan the walk-behind experience for warmer conditions and check local notices before visiting. Ice and falling ice can close the path, especially in winter; respect every closure. The waterfall itself can still be viewed from the front.

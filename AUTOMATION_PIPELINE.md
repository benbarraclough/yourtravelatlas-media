## Outcome and boundary

Run @yourtravelatlas hands-free, using only existing GitHub and Metricool accounts, existing free legitimate sources and existing ChatGPT entitlement, with **£0 additional spend** and no paid integrations/services/subscriptions. Continuously **research, commission, build, independently challenge, verify, schedule and monitor** visually exceptional, informative, inspirational and attention-grabbing original travel content. The metric of success is **live approved posts booked into worthwhile evenly distributed slots**, not completed administrative checks, documents, workflow triggers or counts of candidate ideas.

**No routine owner involvement or approvals.** Ben delegated internal editorial decisions and scheduling on 1 November. Only seek input for a genuinely new artistic direction, monetary commitment, authorisation/connection issue or an unavoidable blocker requiring an owner decision. Report concrete progress or blocker, not routine status requests.

**Monthly budget: maximum 20 posts in a calendar month** across known publication and scheduled entries (and stricter provider/free-account limits if applicable); replace previous internal maximum 16. Cross-check the user's Metricool allowance counter and full available Metricool month entries, plus known manual posts; never treat provider API's limited archive as a complete enumeration of manual content. The owner's reported current allowance use of three must not be assumed to equal the November total: the live 5 November check had three confirmed published and Japan pending (four dated November). Count conservatively, don't invent remaining capacity, do not rely on a failed schedule call to enforce limits. Maximum is a ceiling, not a demand for low-quality content.

**Cadence:** target **4 strong posts/week**, up to **5 when the month's genuine remaining allowance, quality and coverage justify it**, evenly spread across weeks. Prefer Mon 19:00, Wed 12:00, Fri 19:00, Sun 17:00 Europe/London, using other spaced slots for an eligible fifth and avoiding slots already occupied. This supersedes the old Mon/Wed/Thu three/week default; preserve the existing Japan slot Mon 9 Nov 19:00. A Reel replaces a slot; it never adds one for the same story. Do not produce close copies or repeat destinations/themes merely to fill a grid. No need to artificially reserve unused capacity; distribute month-wide. If next 7 days are empty, fix the earliest eligible date first without sacrificing any quality gate.

**Buffer:** at least 14 days of **already approved and scheduled future posts**, and preferably a month planned out where capacity allows; this is a **lead-time/resilience target, not a two-week cap or publication frequency**. Separately maintain a backlog of researched candidates for at least the next 2–3 weeks. Never call a planned idea “scheduled”.

## Four separate jobs, shared evidence and responsibilities

All jobs read this document and the current GitHub state plus the entire relevant canonical editorial standards; use live tools, not a remembered snapshot. Persistent coordination is through GitHub files/reviews, not messages in individual automation chats. Respect optimistic concurrency with fresh file SHA reads. Jobs must not simultaneously edit the same file; re-fetch and merge rather than erase someone else's stage/evidence. Each is independently useful and may continue other approved work when an edition is blocked.

1. **Research & Commission** — Sunday morning and Wednesday morning: inspect latest queue and past posts, existing approvals, budget, diverse external inspiration (incl. hospitality.guide actual accessible content with honest inspection scope, LOST iN/Along Dusty Roads and other publications as applicable), and primary destination information. Produce **one distinctly strong, researched commission-ready idea per run** when supportable. Compare at least 3 genuinely different concepts and at least 2 accurate hooks; explain the winner, audience payoff, save/share reason, visual availability and recent-account differentiation. Create concise stored briefs with sourced facts, rights candidates, geographic/energy/formats variety and approval verdict for stage 1. Research the actual named essentials, not generic tourist clichés; use source URLs and checked date. Do not spend whole run just documenting a full queue, and do not create superficial unverified ideas to reach a quota.
2. **Media Production** — Sunday early afternoon and Thursday morning: take the oldest/most urgent eligible commissions, confirm the stage-1 evidence, research and clear exact media/claim rights, storyboard/caption, create actual content/posts or reel briefs, render using supported GitHub tooling, check outputs/errors/brief hashes. Aim to produce **one genuinely complete candidate edition per run** as resources allow. Fix routine implementation issues and seek legitimate free alternatives. Record rendered artefacts, hashes, licences, source URL/author/location, per-slide/clip purpose, caption/credits, tested render status and remaining QA items. Stage is `RENDERED_PENDING_QA`, NEVER PASS or scheduled simply because a workflow exited 0. No fake AI scenery, copied competitor assets, made-up locations, generic weak imagery or paid APIs.
3. **Quality, Revision & Scheduling** — Monday and Friday mornings: independently critically examine each candidate against all seven gates, including **actual visual inspection of EVERY final slide at full and phone scale or entire Reel motion**, physical image/video consistency, meaningful caption and subject/claims/rights, weakest slide/shot, comparison to examples, interest/variety, no music, verified silent MP4/no audio streams, and fresh manifest/brief checks. Rework failures when realistically possible; otherwise mark REVISE/REJECT/BLOCKED with precise evidence for production and continue to another candidate. Only exact-edition PASS can be imported into Metricool. Before scheduling fetch current month and provider capacity; choose evenly spaced earliest genuine vacancies through the month; preserve existing UUIDs on replacement; re-fetch exact media count/order, caption, alt text, date, provider status PENDING, draft=false, autoPublish=true, no duplicates. Aim to schedule **one approved edition per review run**, and any other already fully PASS editions only if monthly capacity and spacing permit if ready; report a specific missed-stage issue, not “research incomplete” as the only result. Update publication records and future queue. Do not falsely claim a render was viewed or a Reel watched when that inspection is unavailable; record the blocker and keep it unscheduled.
4. **Recovery, Capacity & Learning** — Saturday morning: reconcile actual Metricool publish/failure statuses and future vacancies, monthly counter/unknown manual entries, GitHub unfinished stages, blocked/failed builds, publication/caption/image discrepancies, duplicate ideas, geographic/format variety, and real available saves/shares/follows/reach/watch metrics. Retry routine technical repairs and finish any truly ready QA/scheduling only after the same strict gates. Prioritise upcoming seven days and trigger concrete pipeline handoff records for next research/production. Do not simply produce a status memo. Notify Ben if there is a material ongoing blocker, repeated delivery failure, new authorisation requirement or no approved content available despite recovery. Do not create extra posts to compensate for low performance.

A ChatGPT completed execution is not a completed publishing deliverable. ## Editorial non-negotiables carried over from all project decisions

- Exactly three complementary formats: **formal Faroe-inspired Travel Discovery Collection** (real destination guide, signature sights + enough excellent real imagery, actions and plausible plan); **original hook-led Discovery carousel** (non-obvious engaging honest selection: unusual places, best-of with criteria, animals, film sites, local weekends, rail, road trips, natural and cultural experiences); **silent authentic-video Travel Reel** (minimum 5 truly distinct strong shots, coherent visual hook, context, progression, information, payoff). Vary formats, geography, trip accessibility, seasons, ambition and environments. Long-run ~40% easy exploration / 35% approachable journeys / 25% demanding adventure is a flexible balance, not a rigid per-week quota. Reels generally around fortnightly but adjust for genuine quality and diversity, never forced.
- Compelling clear title/hook visible on cover; impressive varied, well-framed REAL properly licensed photos/video; restrained professional readable editorial typography, small @yourtravelatlas, clear destination identity, phone-size legibility. Model hospitality.guide's succinct readable presentation but **do not copy** competitors. Avoid identical templates, gimmicks and generic “inspiring” waffle. Japan is a positive reference. The published Faroe issue is the formal guide design anchor. Porto demanded more genuinely worthwhile places/images, not a padded caption. Riviera rail must actually visibly show a train; verify each activity's visual premise.
- For carousel current working bounds 5–10 pages incl exactly one distinct scenic final follow card; each worthwhile supporting page is exact location, precise experience and payoff or specific fact, supporting overlay ≤30 editorial words incl titles/context. Numerical promises must match actual supported item count and not count cover/CTA as items. Caption ≤2,200 chars INCLUDING credits/hashtags, with original engaging opening, concrete value and 📍 exact locations; if a collection is not a continuous route, explicitly say so. Festival dates, source facts, seasonal advice and access limitations genuinely checked, never invented. Travel worth going for, not just list of stock assets.
- Reels: authentic licensed real motion, at least five genuinely different strong clips (not duplicate trims), portrait framing quality, actual final-motion review, purposeful text in a legible lower-mid band (Iceland approved placement), clear destination near top where applicable, no clip counter, small branding, a genuinely useful story and caption. **NO MUSIC ON ANYTHING**; final reel MP4 must contain zero audio streams. No Wander Further text.
- **NO PUBLIC ISSUE NUMBERS on any content.** Retain internal stable identifiers. No unapproved over-designed boxes/icons, excessive text, tiny overlays, AI-generated fake landscapes, misleading stock, inaccessible rights, invented itinerary detail, fabricated experience or claims that viewed unviewed competitor content.
- For each edition record competing concepts and competitor inspo with exact inspection scope; verified primary facts; item-by-item sources and licenses/creator/actual exact location; signature-place coverage matrix for Collection; page/clip contributions and rejected weak alternatives; at least two hook options; rendered image or movie hashes; mobile-size visual inspection evidence; separate challenge of weakest element; seven-gate PASS/REVISE/REJECT/BLOCKED record. A self-assigned score alone is insufficient. Final approval of the exact revision only. Old approvals become invalid if core images/caption change.
- Source restrictions, authorisation limits and host tests in `FOOTAGE_SOURCES.md` apply. For video search beyond one library as legitimately available; no bypassing 403/429 or fake licences. Do not simply screenshot another publisher's reel. Rendered success is not approval.
- Keep public account non-promotional and accessible; no pretence of personal experience, paid placement or sponsored hotels. Spend exactly £0 beyond already enabled entitlement. Do not change final logo/account identity without actual owner approval.
- Scheduled ≠ published. Preserve already-approved and live posts, dates, exact IDs, rights and committed source evidence; change as needed only for verified corrections. Avoid content/reel duplicate replacement slots. No routine owner request, no daily admin burden on Ben.

## Robustness rules

On any run, inspect current connected account/repo truth before acting. Do not rely on old `CURRENT_STATE` timestamps, prior automation summaries, published dates that are now in the past, or stale post IDs. Reconcile twice when scheduled: before and after. Never send a materially false “complete” status if no deliverable was produced. Inability to finish every edition is okay **if** a genuinely independent other item was advanced and each blocker and handoff is persisted; do not silently advance the stage without its mandatory evidence.

Use GitHub actions only for the files supported by existing workflows; workflows run on push/dispatch, they do not publish to Instagram. Metricool handles scheduled Instagram publishing. No workflow success overrides source/creative QA. If necessary to create an additional stage-specific GitHub helper script, ensure correct free/permission/compliance/test behaviour before claiming it's live. Do not schedule unreviewed outputs.

**Success signal** every Sunday: next 7 days continuously populated with genuinely approved posts at the chosen cadence, ideally 14+ days scheduled, publication statuses checked, and transparent usage count respecting 20/month and actual account limits. If absent, name exactly which stage failed and how it will be repaired; repeat failure should not be concealed or deferred until another Thursday.

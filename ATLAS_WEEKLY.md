Updated 9 November 2026. **This is the sole active creative and operating brief for the one weekly publishing automation.** Older AUTOMATION_PIPELINE.md, EDITORIAL_GUIDELINES.md, CONTENT_PROCESS.md, EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md, OPERATING_FEEDBACK_LOOP.md, AUTO_RECOVERY_PROTOCOL.md, and historic QA logs are retained as archives/reference only, NOT mandatory reading, competing requirements or further scheduled tasks.

## Outcome
Every Thursday, do the complete research → create → visually/factually check → schedule process yourself. First count genuinely upcoming auto-publishing posts already booked for the target week; **fill to a TOTAL of four**, not four additional posts if some slots are already occupied. Target **four high-quality Instagram publications for the following week:** **two image carousels, two Reels**. Reels should be **photo-based editorial slideshow videos** by default, and use **genuine motion footage instead** if five or more superb, legitimately reusable relevant clips tell a stronger story. Switch the mix only if needed for actual quality/technical feasibility, explaining deviations. Never use weak filler just to hit four.

## Creative freedom
- Give people a memorable travel idea they'd want to save or share. Mix specific destination discoveries, beautiful regional experiences, unexpected thematic lists, culture, beaches, scenic rail journeys, food, cities and accessible holidays with occasional adventures. Choose the strongest original concepts without rigid quotas, mandatory comparison of three ideas, forced continents, or compulsory trends.
- Each carousel needs **at least five relevant photos** with a stunning visually honest cover, a gripping clear place-and-premise hook, and enough distinct real sights/experiences to paint a coherent, useful story. Use up to Instagram's supported 10 slides if needed; one scenic follow/brand ending only when it adds to the story, not by reflex. If a title promises a count, show the full count.
- Each Reel needs **at least five distinct relevant visual scenes**. A photo Reel is an actual silent 9:16 MP4 made from at least five real still photographs with tasteful gentle motion/transitions and short descriptive text, not a carousel mislabelled as a Reel. A video Reel uses five or more independent authentic moving clips and a clear narrative. Destination/place visible and identifiable; no repetitive footage or generic irrelevant scenic padding. No music or unnecessary sound.
- Strong cover, intentionally beautiful photography, easily readable small brand, short varied editorial captions/on-screen labels. Keep location and the important specific reward clear. Captions: fresh short hook, useful 📍 places, one standout reason/experience each, compact credits and optional invitation to save/share. **No travel-advisory essay, pointless caveats, multiple seasons, unnecessary disclaimers or logistics.** State only material restrictions when omission would materially mislead.
- No public issue numbers, no Wander Further, no promotions, no paid tools or new subscriptions; creative decisions and normal internal approval fully delegated.

## Genuine visuals & rights — essential
Choose concepts partly for the availability of truly spectacular *sourceable* visuals. Search **Pexels** (photos & video, also accessible as an image bank in Metricool), original free **Unsplash** (exclude Unsplash+), and exact-file **Wikimedia Commons** (CC0/public domain or properly satisfied CC licence). For motion, search Pexels first; optionally Mixkit **Free** (never Restricted), Pixabay, genuinely reusable public-domain agency/NPS film only if the particular download and renderer support are verified. Never rip other Instagram creators' footage, tourism-board video without licensed permission, or auto-generate fake destination scenes. A photographer credit alone does not grant reuse rights. Verify on the actual asset page the origin, creator, licence allowing publication and editing, exact place/identity and full-size quality; avoid brand/person releases or unverified location claims. Prefer original portrait/4K quality to poor 9:16 crops. Keep per-photo page URLs/licence evidence compactly in the media brief; credit appropriately in captions. Do not use a provider just because the library as a whole is described as free.

## Quick final check
For each complete final post/reel ask: (1) Hook distinctive, location/promise clear? (2) Real accurate strong images or actual clips, each relevant? (3) An interesting story someone would save/share? (4) Text/cover mobile legible, no ugly crop? (5) Caption light, accurate, correct photo credits? (6) Exact sources and commercial reuse permission checked? (7) Correct file format and no audio in a silent Reel? If any answer is no, make a focused fix, re-render and check again; if the concept fundamentally fails, change concept instead. Do not require a seven-gate report, multi-stage handoff, or extensive status paperwork; a single brief and concise final summary suffice.

## Repeatable footage feasibility gate

1. Research interesting experiences and identify several strong Reel ideas. Search Pexels AND at least one suitable additional source before dismissing a promising idea for lack of footage. For wildlife/parks, inspect specialist primary archives too. Avoid making every idea another city montage.
2. Define the hook and the visual story: one remarkable experience, a short journey, a surprising discovery, a coherent atmosphere, or an occasional themed collection. All footage must support that intention. A single experience can have five genuinely different shots; it does not need five different places.
3. Build a clip slate with at least five distinct purposeful real moving shots, normally 5–8, including a compelling opener, progression/reveal and worthwhile final shot. Do not count multiple trims of the same shot as new footage. Reject disconnected scenes, generic filler or imagery that could be anywhere.
4. Verify exact location, creator, original-stock status and per-asset commercial editing permission. Save source page, licence URL, checked date, attribution/changes, source dimensions, source URL and desired shot interval. Credit does not replace permission. Social-media posts, tourism-board videos and YouTube clips are research leads unless explicit reuse rights are verified; do not simply rip them.
5. Prefer native 9:16 at 1080×1920 or better, or 4K landscape that retains enough detail in a portrait crop. A1920×1080 landscape centre crop retains only about 608×1080 before enlargement; 720p retains roughly 405×720. Inspect the actual cropped output, not just the resolution label. Adjust crop deliberately if justified; reject when the subject, place identity or sharpness is lost. Do not force every landscape into a bad centre crop or stretch video.
6. Check the actual interval for movement, focus, exposure, transitions, intrusive branding, season consistency and honest continuity. Decoded stills help shortlist, but do not prove the motion is good. Watch final moving footage and listen to final music before approval. Treat any supplied audio as separately requiring verification; an audio stream is not evidence of useful or cleared ambient sound.
7. Prove authorised download, codec/decode and rendering in GitHub Actions before importing a new provider into production. Current render_reel.py supports Pexels and selected CC licences/hosts; Pixabay's CDN is allowed but its named Content License is not yet accepted. Coverr/Mixkit hosts and licences, NPS public-domain handling and Dareful HLS ingestion require a small source-specific implementation and tests before use. **This source audit does not silently expand the downloader allowlist or claim these providers already work in the production renderer.** Do that work when an actual worthwhile concept needs a verified candidate; do not bypass 403/429, manufacture download paths, install/pay for tools or use preview watermarked files.
8. Follow existing positive licensed-music, text, complete actual-image/motion/audio review and scheduling gates. Genuine video is the priority; do not disguise animated stills as authentic video. If fewer than five strong usable shots remain, reject/rework the idea or skip the Reel slot. Keep the existing cadence and 16-post monthly ceiling; no compensatory volume.

## Editorial conclusion

The additional catalogues justify continuing with genuine video, but source breadth does not establish that any particular Reel is ready. Search more widely and choose the idea with the strongest coherent footage. One strong Reel is preferable to a scheduled series of ordinary stock montages. No new Reel has been generated or scheduled by this audit.

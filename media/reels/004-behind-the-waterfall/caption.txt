Most waterfalls give you a view. This one lets you step inside it. 🇮🇸

📍 Seljalandsfoss, South Iceland
A 60-metre drop beside the Ring Road, with a footpath into the rock recess behind the falling water. The moment to come for: looking out across the plain through the curtain of spray.

Bring a waterproof layer and shoes with grip. The spray reaches the path, so protect your phone before stepping behind the falls.

Plan the walk for ice-free conditions and follow the signs on arrival. Winter ice can close the path; the front view is still worth the stop. Never pass a closure for the photo.

📍 Nearby: Gljúfrabúi, partly hidden by its canyon—another waterfall to add to your South Coast map.

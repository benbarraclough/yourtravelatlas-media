Current owner-directed standard, 30 October 2026. The complete format definitions and flows are in [CONTENT_PROCESS.md](CONTENT_PROCESS.md). Mandatory approval gates are in [EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md](EDITORIAL_REVIEW.md). These three documents supersede v6's single-format discovery-first policy. Historical files are evidence of decisions, not competing current instructions.

## Three permanent editorial formats

1. **Travel Discovery Collection:** formal, Faroe-style photographic destination guides. Help the reader know what to do, what not to miss and how the outings fit a plausible trip. Retain formal masthead/rules/destination hierarchy/serif identity. Internal Collection tracking IDs only. Faroe is Collection 001's visual reference; do not modify the published asset.
2. **Discovery carousels:** gripping honest hooks, exceptional images and concise specific descriptions, inspired by hospitality.guide's readability. Lists, facts, routes, animals, parks, villages, film locations and occasional weekend ideas. No public issue number on new editions. Full practical itineraries are not mandatory.
3. **Travel Reels:** authentic video with a strong visual hook, coherent progression, useful information and silent exports. Minimum five genuinely different purposeful shots, normally 5–8. A short scenery montage with a title and CTA fails. No public issue or clip counter. The current five-shot Iceland edition and lower-middle centred typography received owner approval.

Destination guides must visibly include essential sights/signature experiences as well as discoveries. Record essentials included, omitted and why. Better captions do not replace missing places or photography. A rail/road/activity premise must visibly show its transport/activity in a meaningful asset. Porto and Riviera remain visual revisions; Japan is a positive editorial reference.

Every new brief declares content_type, reader promise, scope, distinctive angle, save/share reason and effort/accessibility where relevant. The format must match the reader's need. Keep one recognisable publication, with different editorial jobs and appropriate presentation.

## Shared standards

- Discover interesting travel experiences first. Compare at least three concepts. Use varied accessible social, editorial, travel-book and destination sources as inspiration; record exact inspection scope and access limitations. Verify claims independently through primary evidence. Never claim to have viewed an inaccessible post/video or read an unseen book.
- Real, accurately identified, properly licensed, high-quality photographs and moving footage only. No fake AI destinations, watermarks, unlicensed competitor assets or undisclosed promotions. A source URL, licence string or successful download is not enough. Prefer no publication to weak or misleading material.
- Supporting carousel overlays: absolute 30 editorial words including headings, dates and archive/context labels, usually fewer. Collection depth comes from specific actions and trip coherence, Discovery depth from distinctive information. Do not shrink type to cram more words. New covers must have a clear promise; the Collection alone retains the formal hierarchy.
- Carousels: currently 5–10 total pages including exactly one scenic follow card. Page count follows value. Numerical promises must deliver every item. Larger formats require current platform/delivery checks and tested code support. Do not claim a format is supported merely because it is written in a playbook.
- Final carousel card: “Fill your feed with wonder.” / “Follow @yourtravelatlas for beautiful places and the details that turn them into real plans.” Use a distinct beautiful licensed image. No false “follow now or never see this again” claim. No WANDER FURTHER on new content.
- Caption: draft two strong accurate openings; choose the better one. Include 📍 exact places mapped to pages or video order and the critical information appropriate to the premise. Keep it readable and within the existing2200-character production ceiling including credits/hashtags. A pin is a text location marker, not an interactive geotag. No fabricated firsthand experience, prices, timings, access or guaranteed sightings/weather.
- Evergreen by default: verified month/season information, with exact programmes/dates checked when necessary. Festivals are optional and must strengthen the concept. Do not imply an event lasts all month or that archive images depict a future edition. Essential context stays on screen when its omission would mislead; fuller logistics go in the caption.
- All imagery, crop, overlay, caption and sources are reviewed together. Every page/shot must add value, context, specific detail or a purposeful visual reveal; the post must contain real substance overall. Do not invent useful detail to justify an attractive but irrelevant asset.
- NO MUSIC on any format. Stop music research/auditions. Reels export with no audio stream; verify this with FFprobe. Watch motion and review text; an audio-listening gate is inapplicable to these silent exports.

## Variety, cadence and cost

Keep a flexible long-run travel-energy mix of roughly 40% easy exploration, 35% journeys/approachable experiences and 25% demanding adventure. Rotate continents, environments, trip scale and intention. A suggested two-week format balance is two Collections, three Discoveries and one Reel, within the established cadence; it is not a quota. Skip weak content.

Retain approximately three weekly publishing slots: Monday 19:00, Wednesday 12:00, Thursday 09:00 Europe/London. A Reel approximately once a fortnight replaces a carousel slot. Never increase volume to compensate for rejection. £0 additional cost, no paid APIs/upgrades/new integrations without authorisation. Internal hard cap16 monthly publications across networks and formats; do not rely on the previously recorded Metricool Free 20 entitlement staying unchanged—check account constraints if uncertain. Count published, pending and manual posts across the entire target month before scheduling.

## Approval and stability

Routine posts use the calibrated autonomous internal review. Explicit owner review requests remain binding; the current Istanbul replacement requires owner approval of the exact finished video and caption. “Not bad … could definitely be improved” is revision feedback, not approval. New framework documentation is not asset approval.

Apply PASS / REVISE / REJECT with evidence at every mandatory gate. Inspect exact final assets, retain revision/hash/source records and resolve all blockers. A checklist, attractive cover or technical success cannot approve an uninspiring post. Schedule pinned reviewed media; re-fetch Metricool to verify the full result. Never describe PENDING as published.

Stable format definitions should survive individual feedback. Improve within them; fundamental changes to identity, cadence or approval ownership need an explicit owner decision. Preserve already published and approved scheduled assets. Owner authorised cleanup of obsolete content; 28 obsolete files were removed. Future cleanup must preserve live dependencies, source evidence and recovery history. Read current queue/repo state; do not reuse stale hardcoded “next issue” numbers.

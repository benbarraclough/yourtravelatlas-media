## Promise / storyboard (6 pages: cover + four substantive stops + distinct scenic follow card)
Cover: visually powerful animal in a correctly documented European landscape, not a fifth encounter. Clear headline, tiny handle, no number/issue label.
1. **Skomer Island, Pembrokeshire, Wales — Atlantic puffins**. Puffins around mid-April to late July, island landings subject to booking/weather; describe close observation from marked paths, not guaranteed access. https://www.welshwildlife.org/visit/skomer-island
2. **Postojna/Notranjska, Slovenia — wild brown bears from a guided forest hide**. Evening hide experience; sightings not guaranteed, guided/welfare-first, season commonly mid-May to September for a named local operator; do not imply free unguided viewing. https://postojnsko.si/en/poi/bear-watching/ ; https://www.zelenikras.si/en/experience/bear-watching-in-the-forests-of-notranjska/
3. **Lake Saimaa, Finland — Saimaa ringed seal**. Small-group lake safari from Oravi or Puumala, timing differs by operator; May particularly useful for basking, some tours run June–September; never promise a sighting. https://www.visitfinland.com/en/product/40dab70c-4f31-40a1-8940-81b3c80e3b98/sunset-seal-safari/ ; https://www.visitfinland.com/en/prodotto/5b23c936-565d-43bc-b706495adfd278dd/saimaa-seal-high-season-safari-by-eco-boat-in-puumala/
4. **Monfragüe National Park, Extremadura, Spain — griffon and black vultures**. View from designated viewpoints/observatories, no guaranteed individual species sighting; park visitor centre in Villarreal de San Carlos. https://turismocoria.es/turismo/en/explore-the-surroundings/monfrague-national-park/ ; https://extremadurasenderismo.juntaex.es/es/servicios/oficinas-de-turismo/detalle/Centro-de-visitantes-del-Parque-Nacional-de-Monfraguee-00003/
Final: different licensed scenic image, required “Fill your feed with wonder.” CTA.

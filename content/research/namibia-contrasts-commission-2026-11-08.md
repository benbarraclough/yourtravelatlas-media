## Reader promise, emotional idea and hooks

**Reader promise:** discover unexpectedly different and real things worth visiting across Namibia, with a distinct location, experience and point of interest per page. A real desire to travel, not random pretty pictures.

**Cover hook A (preferred):** "Namibia has dunes that touch the ocean. That's only the beginning."
**Cover hook B:** "A ghost town swallowed by sand. A desert at the sea. Discover Namibia."
**Alternative brief:** "Namibia: six places that don't look real" is punchy, but use only if exactly six actual featured places are delivered; recommended A does NOT make a numerical promise. Keep @yourtravelatlas discreet and the cover image exceptional.

**Share/save reason:** each swipe reveals a very different real environment; caption tells readers the exact places to save, what they can do there, plus what is relatively accessible versus requiring long journeys. Warm editorial energy and a varied continent choice (not all high-adrenaline adventures).

**Format plan (seven pages total: cover + five supports + closing card):**
- Cover: **Sandwich Harbour** — huge dunes directly beside the Atlantic, real aerial/oblique photograph. Actual cover subject must be verified. Hero promise above. Don't pretend every page is on a single route.
- Page 2: **Deadvlei, Sossusvlei area** — dark old camel-thorn tree silhouettes in a white clay pan backed by giant dunes; show actual trees/white pan, not just generic red dunes. Overlay gives specific visual reason; caption gives access context only once fact checked.
- Page 3: **Spitzkoppe** — walk among dramatic granite boulders and a natural arch, different texture/scale from dunes; confirm landmark photograph exact identification.
- Page 4: **Kolmanskop** — deserted mining town where desert sand fills old interiors, visually distinct human/cultural history; images must show the actual sand-filled structures, not unrelated desert ruins.
- Page 5: **Cape Cross** — Cape fur seal colony on Namibia's Atlantic coast, clear identifiable animals and coast; visits and wildlife vary, avoid guaranteed animal-number language. Visual/copy should convey a specific wildlife experience.
- Page 6: **Etosha National Park** — waterholes and wildlife across a vast salt-pan landscape; actual photo must depict verified Etosha, don't promise specific sightings.
- Page 7: **Distinct scenic follow card** (licensed Namibia view, ideally dunes/glorious dusk not repeated scene) with established "Fill your feed with wonder" / "Follow @yourtravelatlas..." treatment; not counted as a sixth place.

**Overlay approach:** place + one specific interesting experience/reward, under 30 words INCLUDING title. Do not use a cramped list or panels. Carefully vary image colour/scale and crop. Caption should identify 📍 each stop in order, open with a strong concise line, make clear these are **widely separated regions, not a weekend or point-to-point itinerary**, give useful access note for Sandwich Harbour (experienced local 4WD driver) and Etosha designated roads, and preserve accurate creator credits and licence links within 2,200 chars. Caption preview idea (not final approved copy): "In Namibia, dunes meet the Atlantic, diamond-era houses fill with sand and a white clay pan holds trees against rust-red dunes. Here are six very different places to build a future trip around. 🇳🇦". Check final facts & any event/season details before writing; no fabricated eyewitness voice.

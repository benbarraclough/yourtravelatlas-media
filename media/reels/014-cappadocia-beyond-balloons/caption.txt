Cappadocia is famous for the balloons. But look at everything underneath them. 🎈

📍 Göreme, Cappadocia, Türkiye

At sunrise, hot-air balloons lift above valleys carved into extraordinary shapes. On the ground, travellers walk through the launch fields, fairy chimneys rise from the rocks, horses roam the sunlit landscape and the valley opens up in spectacular aerial views.

Would you explore on foot or see it all from the air?

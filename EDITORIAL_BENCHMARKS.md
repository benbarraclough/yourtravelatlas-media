These indexes may rewrite descriptions or add metadata: do not attribute their exact prose, ratings or suggested itineraries to the original creator. Mapped place counts do not prove original slide counts.

**Supported editorial lesson (inference from the indexed themes):** a clear, surprising curatorial lens makes a collection more memorable than 'nice places in X'. The theme supplies a reason to keep swiping and send the collection to a like-minded friend.

**Your Travel Atlas improvement:** combine a strong lens and real photographic taste with precise experiences and trip-building detail on the slides. Explain why this particular place belongs, what to do there and a verified detail the reader can use. Do not infer this is absent from hospitality.guide; it is the required improvement to our own earlier generic slides. Larger coherent collections can work; do not treat five images as the default.

## Complementary primary references

- LOST iN: https://lostin.com/pages/about . Their own description emphasises neighbourhood/cultural character and curated local perspective rather than an exhaustive landmark checklist. Adapt deeper street-level specificity, not claims of insider or firsthand expertise we do not have.
- Along Dusty Roads: https://www.alongdustyroads.com/the-road-trip-guides . The actual page offers day-by-day and duration-specific itineraries, stays, routes and planning decisions. Adapt the connection between inspiration and plausible sequencing. Do not copy the authors' firsthand narrative.

## How this changes our production

| Weak earlier habit | Required improvement |
| --- | --- |
| Same short 'wander + café' overlay everywhere | Experience, named essentials, distinct detail and useful decision |
| Five photos chosen because they exist | Storyboard first; add/cut according to actual new value |
| A rail idea that is just a list of towns | Correct direction, one-base/moving-base decision, event-first planning |
| Pretty city scenes with no reason to time the trip | Research real festivals and seasonal hooks; include only when strong |
| Identical layout for every kind of page | Shared identity with distinct story/event/route/planning treatments |
| Generic inspirational caption | Strong accurate hook + concrete saved-plan value beyond slide repetition |
| No clear end/follow proposition | One consistent photographic closing card with an honest value promise |

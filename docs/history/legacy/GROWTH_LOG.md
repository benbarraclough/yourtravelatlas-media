### Experiment A — reader-useful micro-payoff on each curated-list slide (PROPOSED; editorial handoff recorded)
**Hypothesis:** For a new curated list, replacing generic destination praise with one **concrete why-go/when/how insight** per item will give more reason to save and share. This aligns with existing Gate 3 and 30-word overlay cap; no template change or new assets required.
**Action:** Research and production of `006-europe-wildlife-encounters` should test one clear visual label + one short distinctive, verified visitor payoff per item, while preserving truthful species, location, timing, access and rights. E.g. do not say an animal sighting is guaranteed; only use verified viewing seasons. QA must reject superficial, repetitive or overloaded overlays. This is a low-risk refinement, not permission to bypass the existing workflow. Next research commission may also use the approach if 006 cannot clear rights.
**Measurement:** After a properly QA-approved list is actually published, capture per-post saves/reach and shares/reach at approximately 7 and 14 days where available; compare descriptively with the nearest broadly comparable published carousel and track whether data is missing. Small n means no causal inference. **Baseline unavailable**: post-level saves/shares not returned in this run. Stop/revise if factual nuance or visual readability suffers. Research/production own the test; QA owns pass; growth reviews after 23 Nov if an eligible test has published. **Not launched** as of 9 Nov because 006 is not rendered.

### Experiment B — differentiated cover promise and first caption sentence (PROPOSED; low-risk test)
**Hypothesis:** Clearer reader-benefit covers plus an immediate concrete first-line payoff may improve first-impression comprehension and future saves/shares, without imitating another account or using generic clickbait.
**Action:** For the next eligible destination commission (not retroactively editing Japan or published content), commission two distinct truthful cover options: (i) simple destination+experience, (ii) destination+specific unusual reward; select by visual truth, concise readability and actual trip value, and write a first caption sentence that adds a specific fact rather than rephrasing the title. Do not invent ranking, season, access or promise. Use one approved cover; this is **sequential editorial testing**, not an Instagram randomized A/B experiment.
**Measurement:** 7/14-day reach and saves/reach if exposed, plus shares/reach when available, logged against similar future destination posts; no causal conclusions without adequate sample. Baseline not established; run only after a complete approved asset exists. Next review no earlier than 23 Nov.
